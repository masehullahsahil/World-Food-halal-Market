About World Foods Halal Market

Our Story

Serving Richfield with Quality Halal Products

World Foods Halal Market is a family-owned grocery store in Richfield. We specialize in fresh halal meats, international groceries, Afghan fresh bread, dry goods, spices, nuts, dates and everyday essentials from around the world.

Our goal is simple: provide useful products, friendly service and a welcoming shopping experience for every customer who walks through our doors.

Fresh Halal Meat

Beef, chicken, lamb and goat at the meat counter, cut to order. Somali-style beef and chicken suqaar are prepared here too.

Afghan Bread Baked Here

Afghan naan is baked in the store through the day, alongside cookies and sweets in the bakery case.

Global Selection

Products from the Middle East, South Asia, Africa and beyond are stocked throughout the store. Selection and availability may vary.

Community First

The store serves Richfield, nearby south Minneapolis and surrounding communities.

EBT/SNAP Accepted

EBT/SNAP is accepted for eligible food items.
