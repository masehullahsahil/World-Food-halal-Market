Privacy

Privacy Policy

How this website handles information, in plain language.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

World Foods Halal Market is a grocery store at 6626 Penn Ave S, Richfield, MN 55423. This page explains what information our website, wfhalal.com, does and does not collect.

This website collects no personal information

This is an informational website. It shows our store hours, departments, products and specials. There are no forms to fill out, no accounts to create, no online ordering, no checkout and no newsletter signup. You can browse every page without giving us your name, email address or any other personal information, and the website does not ask for or store any.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies, and it does not use web analytics, advertising trackers or social media pixels. We do not track which pages you visit, and we do not build profiles of visitors. Like most websites, the service that hosts and delivers these pages may keep basic technical server logs (such as which page was requested and when) to run and secure the site. We do not use those logs to identify individual visitors.

Google Maps and Google Fonts

Two parts of this site are provided by Google:

  • Map. The home page and contact page show a Google Maps map of our store, and the directions links open Google Maps. When the map loads or you open a directions link, Google receives your request and may collect information such as your IP address under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Fonts. The typefaces on this site load from Google Fonts, which also involves a request to Google's servers.

Google's handling of that information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy, not this one.

If you contact the store

If you call us or email us, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your phone number, email address and what you asked about. We use it only to answer you and help with your request. We do not sell it, rent it or add you to a marketing list.

Google reviews

Our store has a listing on Google (Google Business Profile). If you leave a review there, Google shares the review and the public name on your Google account with us through Google's business tools, so store staff can read reviews and reply to them. Staff use internal store systems to help manage this. We do not sell reviewer information and we do not use it for advertising.

Payments in the store

Purchases happen in the store, not on this website. Card and EBT/SNAP payments at the register are processed by our payment providers under their own privacy policies. This website is not connected to in-store purchases.

Changes to this policy

If we change how this website works — for example, if we ever add a form or analytics — we will update this page and the date at the top first.

Questions

Questions about this policy or your privacy: email contact@wfhalal.com, call (651) 252-0042, or visit us at 6626 Penn Ave S, Richfield, MN 55423. We are open daily, 10:00 AM–10:00 PM.
