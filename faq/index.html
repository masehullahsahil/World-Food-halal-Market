Questions

Store Questions, Answered

Information about our location, hours, meat counter, bakery, groceries and EBT/SNAP.

Where is World Food Halal Market located?

We are at 6626 Penn Ave S, Richfield, MN 55423.

What are your hours?

We are open every day from 9:30 AM to 9:30 PM.

Do you sell halal meat?

Yes. Our butcher counter has fresh halal meat, cut to order — beef, chicken, lamb and goat.

Do you bake bread in the store?

Yes. Afghan naan is baked fresh in the store every day.

Do you accept EBT/SNAP?

Yes. EBT/SNAP is accepted.

What groceries do you carry?

Fresh produce, basmati rice, spices, dates and a wide range of international groceries.
