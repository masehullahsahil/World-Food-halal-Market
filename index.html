About Us

A family-owned halal grocery store proudly serving Richfield and south Minneapolis

Our Story

Serving Richfield with Quality Halal Products

World Foods Halal Market is a family-owned grocery store in Richfield, proudly serving nearby south Minneapolis and surrounding communities. We specialize in fresh halal meats, international groceries, Afghan fresh bread, dry goods, spices, nuts, dates and everyday essentials from around the world.

Our mission is simple — provide high-quality halal products, friendly service, and a welcoming shopping experience for every customer who walks through our doors.

🏅

Certified Halal

All meats are hand-slaughtered and certified halal according to halal guidelines.

🌍

Global Selection

Products from the Middle East, South Asia, Africa, and beyond — all in one store.

🤝

Community First

Family-owned and rooted in the Richfield and south Minneapolis community we serve.

💳

EBT / SNAP Accepted

We proudly accept EBT for eligible food items to serve all members of our community.
