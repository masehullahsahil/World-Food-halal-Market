A Halal Grocery Store Built Around the Meat Counter and the Bread Oven

World Foods Halal Market is a family-owned neighborhood grocery on Penn Avenue South, serving Richfield, nearby south Minneapolis and surrounding communities. Halal meat cut at the counter, Afghan bread baked in store, and international groceries for everyday cooking.

Halal Butcher Counter

Fresh halal beef, chicken, lamb and goat. Custom cuts are available at the counter. Selection and availability may vary.

Suqaar and Custom Cuts

Somali-style beef and chicken suqaar, ground beef, kabob cuts, bone-in and boneless portions. Call ahead to check availability or discuss a custom order.

Afghan Naan Baked In Store

Afghan bread is baked in store. Bread is baked in batches through the day, so call ahead to check availability.

Groceries and Everyday Basics

Produce, basmati rice, lentils, spices, dates, nuts, tea and cold drinks. EBT/SNAP is accepted for eligible food purchases.

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to what customers ask most at the counter and on the phone.

Do you sell fresh halal meat?

Yes. Our meat counter carries fresh halal beef, chicken, lamb and goat. Selection and availability may vary.

Can I request custom meat cuts?

Yes. Custom cuts of beef, chicken, lamb and goat are available at the counter. Call ahead to check availability or discuss a custom order.

Do you prepare chicken and beef suqaar cuts?

Yes. Somali-style beef and chicken suqaar cuts are prepared at the counter. Call ahead to check availability.

Is Afghan naan baked in the store?

Yes. Afghan naan is baked in store. Bread is baked in batches through the day, so call ahead to check availability.

What are your hours?

We are open daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Do you accept EBT/SNAP?

Yes. EBT/SNAP is accepted for eligible food purchases.

Where are you located?

We are at 6626 Penn Ave S, Richfield, MN 55423, serving Richfield, nearby south Minneapolis and surrounding communities.

Can I call ahead for a specific product or meat cut?

Yes. Call (651) 252-0042 to check availability or discuss a custom order.

Does product, bread and meat availability change?

Yes. We restock throughout the week and bake in batches, so selection and availability may vary. Call ahead to check availability.
